Common questions

How do I steer?
Hold your iPhone sideways like handlebars and tilt it left or right. Hold GAS with your right thumb to go and let go of it to slow down. BOOST sits in the middle and FIRE under your left thumb.
Where's the brake?
There isn't one any more. Let go of GAS and the bike slows down by itself, firmly at low speed, and it pulls up beside your passenger at the kerb. The left thumb is for FIRE now. (On a computer the down arrow still brakes.)
Steering is too twitchy or too slow
Open Settings in the game and choose Gentle, Normal or Sharp tilt. Gentle needs the most lean to turn fully; Sharp needs the least.
The bike won't turn when I tilt
Close the game fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again, then hold the phone sideways and tilt. Steering uses the phone's motion sensor and needs no permission.
Why do I stop at the kerb at the start?
With fewer rivals than All, everyone picks up a passenger after the lights. Pull in towards the person with the marker over their head and let go of GAS as you reach them: you'll stop beside them and they hop on. Race against All and it's straight into the racing.
Does anything cost money?
No. Every bike, paint and finish is free from the first ride, and every bike has the fastest engine. There are no purchases, adverts or accounts.
Why can't I hear anything?
Check that your iPhone isn't on silent, then check the Music and Sound settings in the game.
Does it need the internet?
No. The whole game runs on your iPhone, so it works in Airplane Mode. The things that use the internet are live races and crew sharing (rider looks and ghost runs).
How do I share my rider with friends?
Tap Customise under the riders and make your changes. Then tap Start a crew and send the code. Friends tap Customise, then Join a crew, and type the code. Everyone in the crew sees each other's riders. Saving a look needs your iPhone to be signed in to iCloud.
What do the glowing crates do?
In races with rivals, ride through one and the FIRE button (bottom left) spins up something to use: a homing rocket, three rockets, a goo ball, a shockwave or a shield. Tap the button to use it. Anything a rocket hits spins round, cars included. Settings has a switch to turn weapons off.
What's a close call?
Squeezing past a car without touching it: less than about 90 cm between you and its side, while you're passing it at over about 27 mph faster than it's going. It charges your BOOST by five coins' worth (eight if you're passing very fast, which mostly means oncoming traffic). In a Coins round it goes in your pot as well, and counts towards winning.
How do I race a friend live?
On the menu tap Live race and invite them through Game Center (you both need to be signed in to Game Center, in iPhone Settings). When they accept, you're both in the Live race lobby, which shows who's there and whether they're ready. Whoever invited picks the track, everyone picks their own rider and bike, and once everyone has tapped I'm ready the race starts three seconds later. After each race everyone meets back in the lobby; nobody is pulled out of a race they're still riding. The other riders are marked LIVE. You can be anywhere, as long as you're both online.
What's a ghost race?
Tap Ghost race to race the best runs on that track, replayed exactly as they were ridden, as see-through riders marked GHOST with the day they rode. The HUD shows how far ahead or behind the nearest ghost is. Your own best is kept on your iPhone, and if you're in a crew it's shared so your crew can race it, and theirs show up for you.
Why did my race end early?
Every crash costs one of the five hearts in the top corner, and the fifth puts you out.
