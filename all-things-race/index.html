Common questions

How do I steer?
Hold your iPhone sideways like handlebars and tilt it left or right. Hold GAS with your right thumb to go and let go of it to slow down. BOOST sits in the middle and FIRE under your left thumb.
Where's the brake?
There isn't one any more. Let go of GAS and the bike slows down by itself, firmly at low speed, and it pulls up beside your passenger at the kerb. The left thumb is for FIRE now. (On a computer the down arrow still brakes.)
Steering is too twitchy or too slow
Open Settings in the game and choose Gentle, Normal or Sharp tilt. Gentle needs the most lean to turn fully; Sharp needs the least.
The bike won't turn when I tilt
Close the game fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again, then hold the phone sideways and tilt. Steering uses the phone's motion sensor and needs no permission.
What are the bendy roads?
The track list has two sections. Under Straight are the city and open roads, dead straight with the sights either side. Under Bendy are eight country roads that wind, and the dots show how much. A few gentle bends: Sherwood Forest, past the Major Oak, and Horseshoe Pass, round the head of its valley above Llangollen. Moderate bends: Ladybower, round the reservoir; the Cat and Fiddle, up to the inn on the moor; and Winnats Pass, through the limestone gorge and round under Mam Tor. Severe bends and hairpins: Snake Pass, Buttertubs Pass in the Yorkshire Dales and Hardknott Pass in the Lake District. With the steering assist on (in Settings) the bike follows the bends by itself and eases off before the sharp ones, so you just hold GAS and tilt to pick your line. Turn the assist off to take the corners yourself. Pick Championship on any of them to race the roads that are as bendy as that one, in turn.
Why do I stop at the kerb at the start?
With fewer rivals than All, everyone picks up a passenger after the lights. Pull in towards the person with the marker over their head and let go of GAS as you reach them: you'll stop beside them and they hop on. Race against All and it's straight into the racing.
Does anything cost money?
No. Every bike, paint and finish is free from the first ride, and every bike has the fastest engine. There are no purchases, adverts or accounts.
Why can't I hear anything?
Check that your iPhone isn't on silent, then check the Music and Sound settings in the game.
Does it need the internet?
No. The whole game runs on your iPhone, so it works in Airplane Mode. The things that use the internet are live races, crew sharing (rider looks, ghost runs, the weekly cup and crew alerts) and Game Center's leaderboards and achievements.
How do I share my rider with friends?
Tap Customise under the riders and make your changes. Then tap Start a crew and send the code. Friends tap Customise, then Join a crew, and type the code. Everyone in the crew sees each other's riders. Saving a look needs your iPhone to be signed in to iCloud.
What do the glowing crates do?
In races with rivals, ride through one and the FIRE button (bottom left) spins up something to use: a homing rocket, three rockets, a goo ball, a shockwave or a shield. Tap the button to use it. Anything a rocket hits spins round, cars included. Settings has a switch to turn weapons off.
What's a close call?
Squeezing past a car without touching it: less than about 90 cm between you and its side, while you're passing it at over about 27 mph faster than it's going. It charges your BOOST by five coins' worth (eight if you're passing very fast). In a Coins round it goes in your pot as well, and counts towards winning.
How do I race a friend live?
On the menu tap Live race and invite them through Game Center (you both need to be signed in to Game Center, in iPhone Settings). When they accept, you're both in the Live race lobby, which shows who's there and whether they're ready. Whoever invited picks the track, everyone picks their own rider and bike, and once everyone has tapped I'm ready the race starts three seconds later. After each race everyone meets back in the lobby; nobody is pulled out of a race they're still riding. The other riders are marked LIVE. You can be anywhere, as long as you're both online.
What's a ghost race?
Tap Ghost race to race the best runs on that track, replayed exactly as they were ridden, as see-through riders marked GHOST with the day they rode. The HUD shows how far ahead or behind the nearest ghost is. Your own best is kept on your iPhone, and if you're in a crew it's shared so your crew can race it, and theirs show up for you.
What's the daily challenge?
Tap Daily challenge: everyone gets the same track, weather and traffic that day, and there's a new one every day. Your best run is kept as that day's ghost, and if you're in a crew you race your crew's best runs from the same day.
What's the weekly cup?
Tap Weekly cup: three roads for your whole crew, the same three for everyone from Monday to Sunday. Ride each one as a ghost race against your crew's runs. The table adds up everyone's three times, and the lowest total wins the week.
What are crew alerts?
When someone in your crew beats one of your times, the menu says so, with a button to race their ghost. Switch on Crew alerts in Settings and your iPhone sends a notification as well, checking now and then in the background. It asks for permission to send notifications the first time, and you can switch it off again whenever you like.
Where are my trophies and stats?
Tap Trophies on the menu. It shows everything you've done (miles ridden, wins, close calls, fares and the rest), your record against each rider, and 24 trophies to earn. Trophies are only for show: everything in the garage is free already. If you're signed in to Game Center, each trophy is a Game Center achievement too and your best lap on every road goes on that road's leaderboard; the Game Center button on the Trophies page opens them.
What's Elimination?
A race where, every so often, whoever is last is out, until one rider is left. The countdown at the top shows how long until the next knock-out. If it's you, you can watch the rest of the race through another rider's eyes.
Can I keep watching after I'm out?
Yes. If you're out after five crashes, or knocked out in Elimination, while others are still racing, the view moves into another rider's helmet. The arrows swap riders, and Results takes you to the results whenever you like.
What is Changeable weather?
British weather. The race starts in sun, rain or fog and moves between them every half a minute to a minute: the sky, the light, the rain on your visor and the grip under the tyres all change together. In live races and the daily challenge everyone gets the same weather at the same moment.
How do parcel rounds work?
In Drive on a straight road, choose Parcel rounds. Someone at the left kerb has three parcels for you: pull in and let go of GAS to collect them, then ride to each address in turn (the yellow beam at the kerb) and stop there. Every drop pays, and pays more for being quick.
What are the lucky horseshoes?
Five are hidden on every road, out at the edges, always in the same places. Ride through one and it's yours for good. The Trophies page counts how many you've found.
How do I take a picture?
Pause, then tap Photo mode. The buttons and numbers go and the race stays frozen. Take photo keeps the picture, with the game's name in the corner, and opens the share sheet to save it to Photos or send it to someone.
Can I use a game controller?
Yes: an Xbox or PlayStation controller, or any other your iPhone supports, connected over Bluetooth. The stick or d-pad steers, the right trigger or A is GAS, B or X fires, Y or the left shoulder button boosts, the right shoulder button honks and Menu pauses.
Is there an easier way to play for little ones?
Switch on One-thumb mode in Settings. The bike goes by itself: tilt to steer, and hold EASE (where GAS was) to slow down. It still pulls in for passengers, fares and parcels by itself.
What are the faces in a live race?
Four quick reactions. Tap one during a live race and it pops up over your rider on everyone's phone. They're the only messages in the game: there's no chat.
How do moto-taxi fares work?
In Drive, someone waves you down at the left kerb ahead. Pull in and let go of GAS to stop beside them; they hop on and say where they're going. Ride to the drop-off (the yellow beam at the kerb) and stop there. The fare is always paid; the tip is for getting there quickly and smoothly, so crashes, scrapes and red lights cost you.
What's a chain?
Close calls, wheelies and jumps link into a chain when each comes within four seconds of the last. Each one in a chain fills more of your BOOST, up to five times as much. A crash loses the chain.
Why does the bike slide or wobble?
In rain and snow the tyres hold less: swerve hard at speed and they let go for a moment, sooner on painted lines, manhole covers and tram rails. Cross tram rails square on, because at a shallow angle they catch the front wheel. The Lace Market's cobbles shake the bike in any weather.
There's an ambulance behind me
Move out of its lane and it thanks you with a little boost. Stay in its way and it waits behind you, then goes round.
Why did my race end early?
Every crash costs one of the five hearts in the top corner, and the fifth puts you out.
