Common questions

How do I steer?
Hold your iPhone sideways like handlebars and tilt it left or right. Hold BRAKE with your left thumb and GAS with your right; BOOST sits in the middle.
Steering is too twitchy or too slow
Open Settings in the game and choose Gentle, Normal or Sharp tilt. Gentle needs the most lean to turn fully; Sharp needs the least.
The bike won't turn when I tilt
Close the game fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again, then hold the phone sideways and tilt. Steering uses the phone's motion sensor and needs no permission.
Why do I stop at the kerb at the start?
With fewer rivals than All, everyone picks up a passenger after the lights. Pull in beside the person with the marker over their head and stop for a moment. Race against All and it's straight into the racing.
Does anything cost money?
No. Every bike, paint, finish and engine stage is free from the first ride. There are no purchases, adverts or accounts.
Why can't I hear anything?
Check that your iPhone isn't on silent, then check the Music and Sound settings in the game.
Does it need the internet?
No. The whole game runs on your iPhone, so it works in Airplane Mode.
