All Things Race privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

In short

This policy covers the All Things Race app for iPhone, made by Manjinder Boora, an independent developer in the UK. The app does not collect any personal data, and it never asks for any. Only four things can ever leave your iPhone, and only if you use them: the look of riders you customise and your best runs as ghosts, shared with your crew; your rider's moves during a live race with friends; and, if you're signed in to Game Center, your best laps and trophies.

What the app stores

Your chosen rider, bike, paint, track, settings, coins, best times, riding stats (miles, wins, close calls and the like), trophies and the lucky horseshoes you've found are stored only on your iPhone, so the game can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Crew sharing (optional)

You can customise any rider (jacket and helmet colours, hairstyle and hair colour, skin tone, glasses and beard) and share those looks with friends and family in a crew. If you start or join a crew, the looks of the riders you customise, and when you changed them, are saved in the app's iCloud database so that everyone in your crew sees them. Each look is filed under your crew's code and the rider's name.

A look contains nothing about you: no name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. Apple stores the records in iCloud; saving one needs your iPhone to be signed in to iCloud, and iCloud keeps an anonymous ID with each record that doesn't tell us who you are. Anyone with your crew's code can see and change your crew's riders, so share it only with people you know. If you never start or join a crew, nothing leaves your iPhone.

To have a crew's looks deleted, email us the crew code.

Ghost runs (optional)

When you finish a ghost race with your best time on a track, that run is kept on your iPhone so you can race it as a ghost. If you're in a crew it is also saved in the app's iCloud database, filed under your crew's code, the track and the rider's name, so your crew can race it too. A run is where your rider was on the road ten times a second, your time and lap times, the day you rode it, and the rider's look and bike. Like a look, it contains nothing about you, and only someone with your crew's code can find it. Runs in the daily challenge and the weekly cup are shared with your crew in the same way, and the weekly cup's table is made from those times. To have a crew's runs deleted, email us the crew code.

Crew alerts (optional)

If you're in a crew, the game looks up your crew's times (the same iCloud records as the ghost runs: rider names and times) on the roads where you've set a time, and tells you on the menu when someone has gone quicker. If you switch on Crew alerts in Settings, your iPhone also does that check now and then in the background and shows a notification. The notification is made on your iPhone: nothing extra is sent anywhere, there are no push notifications from us, and no notification details reach us. You can switch Crew alerts off in the game's Settings, or notifications off in iPhone Settings.

Live races (optional)

Live races use Apple's Game Center. When you tap Live race you choose friends to invite (never strangers), and Game Center connects your phones. In the lobby and while you race, the phones send each other the rider and bike each person picked, whether they're ready, where each rider is on the road, when they fire something or finish, and any of the four reaction faces they tap. This goes between the phones through Game Center while you race; it isn't stored, and none of it comes to us. The friends you race see your Game Center name. Game Center is Apple's service, under Apple's own privacy policy. There's no chat in the game.

Game Center leaderboards and achievements (optional)

If you're signed in to Game Center, the app sends Game Center your best lap on each road, your longest Endless ride, your daily challenge time and the trophies you earn, so they appear on Game Center's leaderboards and in your achievements under your Game Center name. Game Center decides who can see them, under your Game Center settings and Apple's privacy policy. None of it comes to us. If you aren't signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent.

Photo mode

Photo mode makes a picture of the game's own screen. It stays on your iPhone unless you choose to save or send it from the share sheet. Saving to Photos asks for permission to add that picture; the app can't see or read your photo library.

Game controllers

If you connect a game controller, the app reads its sticks and buttons to ride the bike. Nothing about the controller is stored or sent anywhere.

Motion sensor

You steer by tilting the phone, so the app reads the phone's orientation while you play. That reading is used only to steer the bike in the moment and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Internet

Apart from crew sharing, crew alerts, live races and Game Center, the app makes no network connections. Everything it shows, including the music and sound, is made on your iPhone.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in of its own (live races, leaderboards and achievements use your Game Center account)
  • No purchases
  • No adverts
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone, and no reading of your photos (photo mode can only add a picture you choose to save)
  • No push notifications from a server (a crew alert is made on your iPhone)

Children

The App Store rates the game 9+ because riders can spin each other out with cartoon rockets. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Crew sharing only passes on looks chosen from the game's own lists of colours and styles, and ghost runs; live races are invite-only, and the only messages in them are four fixed reaction faces. There's no chat, no free text and no photo sharing inside the game.

This website
