All Things Race privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

In short

This policy covers the All Things Race app for iPhone, made by Manjinder Boora, an independent developer in the UK. The app does not collect any personal data, and it never asks for any. Only three things can ever leave your iPhone, and only if you use them: the look of riders you customise and your best runs as ghosts, shared with your crew, and your rider's moves during a live race with friends.

What the app stores

Your chosen rider, bike, paint, track, settings, coins and best times are stored only on your iPhone, so the game can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Crew sharing (optional)

You can customise any rider (jacket and helmet colours, hairstyle and hair colour, skin tone, glasses and beard) and share those looks with friends and family in a crew. If you start or join a crew, the looks of the riders you customise, and when you changed them, are saved in the app's iCloud database so that everyone in your crew sees them. Each look is filed under your crew's code and the rider's name.

A look contains nothing about you: no name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. Apple stores the records in iCloud; saving one needs your iPhone to be signed in to iCloud, and iCloud keeps an anonymous ID with each record that doesn't tell us who you are. Anyone with your crew's code can see and change your crew's riders, so share it only with people you know. If you never start or join a crew, nothing leaves your iPhone.

To have a crew's looks deleted, email us the crew code.

Ghost runs (optional)

When you finish a ghost race with your best time on a track, that run is kept on your iPhone so you can race it as a ghost. If you're in a crew it is also saved in the app's iCloud database, filed under your crew's code, the track and the rider's name, so your crew can race it too. A run is where your rider was on the road ten times a second, your time and lap times, the day you rode it, and the rider's look and bike. Like a look, it contains nothing about you, and only someone with your crew's code can find it. To have a crew's runs deleted, email us the crew code.

Live races (optional)

Live races use Apple's Game Center. When you tap Live race you choose friends to invite (never strangers), and Game Center connects your phones. In the lobby and while you race, the phones send each other the rider and bike each person picked, whether they're ready, where each rider is on the road, and when they fire something or finish. This goes between the phones through Game Center while you race; it isn't stored, and none of it comes to us. The friends you race see your Game Center name. Game Center is Apple's service, under Apple's own privacy policy. There's no chat in the game.

Motion sensor

You steer by tilting the phone, so the app reads the phone's orientation while you play. That reading is used only to steer the bike in the moment and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Internet

Apart from crew sharing and live races, the app makes no network connections. Everything it shows, including the music and sound, is made on your iPhone.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in of its own (live races use your Game Center account)
  • No purchases
  • No adverts
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

The App Store rates the game 9+ because riders can spin each other out with cartoon rockets. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Crew sharing only passes on looks chosen from the game's own lists of colours and styles, and ghost runs; live races are invite-only. There's no chat, no free text and no photos.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own privacy policy.
