Take Home privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

In short

This policy covers the Take Home app for iPhone, made by Manjinder Boora, an independent developer in the UK. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does with your information

Every calculation runs on your iPhone. The figures you enter, such as your salary, pension percentage and student loan plan, and your language and appearance choices, are saved only on your iPhone so they are there next time. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can delete the saved figures at any time with "Clear my saved figures" in Settings, or by deleting the app.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No adverts
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs
  • No internet connection needed, and no data sent anywhere
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Links

The app links to GOV.UK pages that list the official rates. Those open in Safari and are covered by GOV.UK's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions about privacy: mboora@outlook.com.
