Relatable privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

In short

This policy covers the Relatable: City Anagrams app for iPhone and iPad, made by Manjinder Boora, an independent developer in the UK. We do not collect any personal data.

What the app stores

Your progress, stars, daily puzzle results, hints, lives and settings are stored on your device. They are never sent to us.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your stars and daily puzzle streak are also saved in your own iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices. This uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage. We can't see or read it. Hints and lives are not synced.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, your total stars, levels solved, best daily streak and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn it off at any time in the Settings app. Relatable works fully without it.

Notifications

If you turn on "Tell me when lives are back", the app schedules a single local notification on your device. It's created by the app itself; nothing is sent to us or to any notification service.

Checking the time

To refill lives fairly, the app asks a public web server (apple.com, google.com or cloudflare.com) for the current time. The request contains no personal information and no identifiers. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that server, which handles it under its own privacy policy. We don't receive or keep it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in of our own
  • No adverts
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions about privacy: mboora@outlook.com.

Relatable is made by Manjinder Boora. It isn't affiliated with any TV programme, broadcaster, council or tourist board.
