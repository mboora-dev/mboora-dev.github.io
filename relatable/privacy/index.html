Relatable privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

In short

This policy covers the Relatable: City Anagrams app for iPhone, made by Manjinder Boora, an independent developer in the UK. The app does not collect any personal data.

What the app stores

Your progress, stars, hints, lives and sound settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

Checking the time

To refill lives fairly, the app asks a public web server (apple.com, google.com or cloudflare.com) for the current time. The request contains no personal information and no identifiers. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that server, which handles it under its own privacy policy. We don't receive or keep it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No adverts
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions about privacy: mboora@outlook.com.

Relatable is made by Manjinder Boora. It isn't affiliated with any TV programme, broadcaster, council or tourist board.
