Common questions

Can I change the order of the cities?
Yes. London, Nottingham, Leicester and Birmingham come first, then the other cities from A to Z. To move a city, press and hold its card (on a Mac, just drag it) and drop it on another city. Your order is saved and follows you to your other devices. To go back to the usual order, open Settings and tap Reset city order.
I bought a city but it's still locked
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.
When do my lives come back?
All three come back at midnight UK time. The game checks the time online, so it needs an internet connection to refill them. You can also restore them straight away in the shop.
What is the daily puzzle?
A new conundrum every day at midnight UK time, picked from all the cities. It's free to play, costs no lives and builds a streak if you solve it every day. Tap Share to post your result without giving the answer away. You can also add the daily puzzle widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, or the Relatable button to Control Centre.
What is a streak freeze?
Every seven days in a row earns one, and you can hold two. If you miss a day, a freeze is used automatically and your streak carries on. Days covered by a freeze are marked with a snowflake in the daily archive.
What is the daily archive?
The last 30 daily puzzles, so you can catch up on days you missed or go back for three stars. It comes with the Every City Pass. Archive puzzles never change your streak.
What is Time Attack?
Two minutes to solve as many anagrams as you can, drawn from the cities you've unlocked. You can skip any that stump you. It costs no lives, and your best score goes on a Game Center leaderboard. On iOS 26 and later you can also challenge friends to beat it.
What is the fact file?
Every answer comes with a fact about the place. Each city keeps the facts for the levels you've solved in its fact file, on the city's level screen.
Does replaying a level cost a life?
No. Once you've solved a level you can replay it as often as you like for free, for example to go for three stars.
Can I play on more than one device?
Yes. Relatable runs on iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and Mac, and the daily puzzle is on Apple Watch. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and your stars and daily streak follow you. Purchases carry over too: tap Restore purchases in Settings on the new device.
Can my family use my purchases?
Yes. Cities and the Every City Pass can be shared through Apple's Family Sharing. Hints and life refills are used up as you play, so they can't be shared.
Can I type my answers?
Yes, on a Mac, or with a keyboard attached to your iPad or iPhone. Type letters to place them, press Delete to take the last one back, Space to shuffle and Escape to clear.
How do I see leaderboards and achievements?
Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then tap the trophy button on Relatable's home screen.
Can it remind me to play?
Yes. In the game's Settings, turn on "Daily puzzle reminder" and pick a time; it skips days you've already solved. "Tell me when lives are back" sends one notification when your lives refill, never overnight.
Can I ask Siri?
Yes. Try "Play today's Relatable", "Start Relatable Time Attack" or "What's my Relatable streak". The same actions are in the Shortcuts app.
I bought the Every City Pass. Do I get new cities?
Yes. The pass unlocks every city, including any added later, at no extra cost.
Why can't I hear anything?
On iPhone and iPad, Relatable stays silent when the device is set to silent. Also check the speaker button in the game and the Sound switches in Settings.
Can I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
I think a clue is wrong
Please email the city, level number and what you think is wrong. Corrections go into the next update.
