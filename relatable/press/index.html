About the game

Pick a city, read the clue and unscramble the letters before the clock runs out. In Nottingham the answers include SHERWOOD and GOOSE FAIR. In Liverpool, SCOUSE and the THREE GRACES. In Belfast, CRAIC and WEE BUNS.

Each city's 100 levels start with familiar words and build to proper local knowledge, with a fact revealed after every solve. Each city keeps the facts you've found in a fact file. Game Center leaderboards, a Home Screen widget, Siri and Shortcuts, iCloud sync, an Apple Watch app, light and dark mode, and original music and sound effects are all included.
